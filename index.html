Kiarash Kiani Feriz

I'm a final-year Computer Engineering undergraduate at Sharif University of Technology. My research asks how machine learning systems fail under adversarial pressure, and how to make them reliable. So far that has meant backdoor attacks and adversarial transferability in speech recognition, robustness of speech foundation models, and anomaly detection in images. I'm advised by Mohammad Hossein Rohban and Mohammad Sabokrou.

Publications

  1. Accepted · NeurIPS 2026

    Breaking Adversarial Transferability in Fine-Tuned Speech Recognition

    Proposes TransferBreaker, a unified fine-tuning framework that mitigates adversarial transferability in black-box ASR deployments.

  2. Accepted · TMLR

    GhostWord: A Fine-Grained Backdoor Attack on Automatic Speech Recognition

    A word-level, temporally localized data-poisoning backdoor that uses acoustic triggers for precise semantic and sentence-level manipulation of ASR output.

  3. Under review · ICASSP 2027

    Transferable Adversarial Robustness for Speech Foundation Models via Hierarchical Stabilization

    Improves adversarial robustness of speech foundation models through hierarchical stabilization, with Mohammad Sabokrou as corresponding author and Mohammad Hossein Rohban as co-advisor.

  4. Under review · ICLR 2027

    Robustness After Specialization: Installing Adversarial Robustness in Speech Foundation Model Descendants

    Studies how robustness can be transferred to specialized descendants of speech foundation models and preserved during downstream specialization. Mohammad Hossein Rohban is the corresponding author, with Mohammad Sabokrou as co-advisor.

  5. Co-authored

    CELAD: Compositional Evaluation for Logical Anomaly Detection

    Logical anomaly detection for industrial images, with results competitive with state-of-the-art methods.

  6. Co-authored

    Generative AI for Character Animation: A Comprehensive Survey of Techniques, Applications, and Future Directions

    A survey spanning face generation, expression rendering, gesture and motion synthesis, avatar creation, and texture modeling.
